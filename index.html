About Me

I am an undergraduate Senior at Case Western Reserve University majoring in Chemistry BS. My research centers on synthesizing stable nanomaterials by integrating insights from materials chemistry and inorganic chemistry. Beyond the lab, I translate my interests in cooking and painting into science, using them to sharpen my sense for structure, color, and transformation.

Research & Academic Focus

My undergraduate lab work focuses on stabilizing formamidine rich perovskites, investigating how procedural choices such as stirring time, injection temperature, and reagent amounts, along with ligand interactions, influence structural stability and optical properties. Each synthesis echoes the artistic process: layering, reacting, and transforming. I examine absorption and emission spectra and quantify quantum yields of the fabricated materials.
