About Me

I’m a Chemistry undergraduate at Case Western Reserve University passionate about material synthesis— discovering how structure, color, and composition interact to create new matter. My curiosity extends beyond the lab: in painting and cooking, I explore the same balance of form and transformation that defines chemical design.

Research & Academic Focus

My research focuses on formamidine-rich perovskites and inorganic nanomaterials, exploring how ligand chemistry influences stability and optical properties. I combine experimental synthesis with creative problem-solving, inspired by how artistry and science share a language of composition.
