About Me

I’m a Chemistry undergraduate at Case Western Reserve University passionate about material synthesis— discovering how structure, color, and composition interact to create new matter. My curiosity extends beyond the lab: in painting and cooking, I explore the same balance of form and transformation that defines chemical design.

Research & Academic Focus

My research centers on formamidine-rich perovskites and inorganic nano-materials, exploring ligand interactions and stability optimization. Through projects at CWRU and Konkuk University, I’ve synthesized lead-free crystals, analyzed absorption and emission peaks, and investigated structure–property relationships.

Art Gallery

Painting allows me to approach chemistry from another angle — mixing pigments and layering textures feels like a visual analog to material design. Each piece reflects experimentation with color blending and reaction between elements.

Culinary Experiments

Cooking is my daily laboratory — where thermal reactions, emulsions, and crystallization reveal chemistry in motion. From handmade pastries to molecular-inspired plating, I approach food with the same creative curiosity I bring to research.

Connecting Science & Art

Whether crafting a perovskite lattice or a layered dessert, the process is guided by the same question: How can structure, energy, and beauty emerge from composition?
